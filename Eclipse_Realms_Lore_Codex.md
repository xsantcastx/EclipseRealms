# Eclipse Realms – Lore Codex

---

## 🌑 The First Dawn
> “In the beginning, there was the Sun — whole, silent, and dreaming.”

Before life, before time, a celestial flame known as the **First Sun** breathed creation into the void.  
Its light birthed the *Luminous Realm*, its shadow gave shape to the *Umbral Realm*.  
From its warmth rose the gods — the **Solarii**, sculptors of order and balance.  

But within that light hid a reflection that hungered.  
A thought unmade: **The First Shadow.**

---

## 🌘 The Shattering
> “When gods wage war, even truth bleeds.”

The Solarii sought to purge the Shadow, but in doing so **shattered the First Sun**.  
Its fragments rained across the world, each piece a prison for a god — or a key to release the darkness they feared.  

Reality splintered. Every being, every soul was mirrored.  
Two realms were born: one bathed in light, one drowned in its echo.  
They exist together, unseen — until the **Eclipse** binds them once more.

---

## 🌗 The Realms

### 🕊 The Luminous Realm
- Land of the living flame.  
- Cities of glass and gold floating above radiant plains.  
- The Solari Church teaches that the Shadow is a lie — a disease of faith.  
- Light energy (“Aether”) fuels miracles and technology.

### 🌒 The Umbral Realm
- A kingdom beneath the ashes of the old world.  
- Twilight forests, obsidian towers, rivers of memory.  
- The Nocturne believe Light is the true corruption — that they preserve reality’s purity.  
- Shadow energy (“Nox”) fuels creation through decay and transformation.

### 🌗 The Verge
- The border between both worlds — unstable, ever-shifting.  
- Where time fractures and echoes of both realms overlap.  
- Convergents — beings born of both lights — wander here, hunted and revered.

---

## 🌔 The Eclipse Cycle
Every **33 years**, the fragments align.  
When the **Eclipse Sun** appears, both worlds merge for seven days.  

During that time:
- Death loses meaning — those slain may return in another form.  
- The First Shadow stirs, whispering through dreams.  
- The **Godforge** awakens — the engine of creation at the world’s heart.  
- The Convergents are drawn to it, seeking purpose — or redemption.  

Whoever claims the Godforge at the height of the Eclipse may **reshape existence**.

---

## 🔥 The Convergents
> “Between light and shadow, the soul remembers both.”

Few are born between realms — their souls neither wholly Light nor Shadow.  
They alone can cross freely, shifting between worlds, but each passage burns away part of their essence.  
Only by binding to **Eclipse Fragments** can they remain whole.  

Legends say a Convergent once sealed the First Shadow — another will decide its fate.

| Faction | Realm | Belief | Goal |
|----------|--------|---------|------|
| **The Solari** | Luminous | The Light is truth; the Shadow must be purged. | Reignite the broken Sun. |
| **The Nocturne** | Umbral | The Shadow is the world’s soul; the Light is rot. | Consume the Light and rule both realms. |
| **The Convergents** | Verge | Balance sustains all. | Prevent total collapse — even if balance means chaos. |
| **The Archivum** | Hidden | Seek truth beyond gods. | Recover the lost knowledge of the First Age. |

---

## 🌕 The Godforge
A colossal structure buried beneath the borderlands.  
Some call it a sun fragment, others a machine built by the gods.  
Its core hums with energy beyond comprehension — *creation itself in mechanical form*.  

During the Eclipse, the Godforge’s gates open, and echoes of every past Eclipse reappear inside it.  
To enter is to face **your own reflections** — the lives you could have lived.

---

## 🌒 The Lost Gods
When the Sun broke, the gods became what they swore to destroy.  
Each now rules a fragment of reality:
- **Aureth**, the Radiant — goddess of order, now bound to the Solari.  
- **Verrin**, the Hollow — god of endings, patron of the Nocturne.  
- **Kael**, the Broken Flame — patron of the Convergents, keeper of the balance.  
- **The Nameless** — believed to be the First Shadow, trapped beneath the world, waiting for the next Eclipse.

---

## 🜂 Prophecy of the Final Eclipse
> “The world shall end not in fire nor shadow,  
> but when the Convergent forgets which one they were.”

Ancient seers foretell a Final Eclipse — when the realms no longer separate but *consume each other*.  
Time will loop, memories will bleed, and the barrier will fail.  

Some believe this will end everything.  
Others whisper it is how the world *heals itself*.

---

## 🩸 The Unwritten Verse
The final page of the Codex is blank — yet faintly warm to the touch.  
When held under the light of a full eclipse, words appear:

> “There was never Light.  
> There was never Shadow.  
> Only the dream of balance…  
> and the nightmare of remembering.”

## 💀 Mythic Artifacts (Lore‑Linked Items)
- **Obsidian Heart:** beats with the pulse of the Nameless. Said to corrupt Light itself.  
- **Mirrorblade of Kael:** exists in both realms; when drawn in one, it vanishes from the other.  
- **Relic of the Third Dawn:** an impossible fragment glowing with both Aether and Nox.  
- **Codex Solarii:** rewritten by those who survive the Eclipse — or cause it.

## 🕯️ Timeline of Ages
1. **The First Dawn** – birth of the Sun, creation of Light and Shadow.  
2. **The Shattering** – war of the gods, birth of dual realms.  
3. **The Age of Mirrors** – humanity adapts; reflections awaken.  
4. **The First Eclipse** – Convergents appear; the Nameless sealed.  
5. **The Age of Fracture** – realms drift apart; myths become religions.  
6. **The Present Eclipse** – fragments align once more; your story begins.

- **Duality:** every truth has a reflection.  
- **Identity:** who are you when both halves disagree?  
- **Sacrifice:** balance demands loss.  
- **Legacy:** every action echoes across realms.
